# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a respectful, harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent while still being accountable for impact.
- Welcome newcomers and help them find useful context.
- Keep discussions focused on the project and its community.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, or discriminatory language.
- Personal attacks or sustained disruption.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Sexualized language or imagery in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, reject contributions, or temporarily or permanently ban participants who violate this code of conduct.

To report a concern, contact the maintainers through the security or maintainer contact process listed for the project. Reports will be handled with care and discretion.
