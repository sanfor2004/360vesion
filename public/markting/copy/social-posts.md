# LinkedIn

Every space. A new perspective.

I've been building 360Vision: a local studio for creating interactive 360°
real-estate tours.

The workflow connects panorama scenes, clickable hotspots, and optional
multi-floor plans. The viewer keeps room navigation compact, and projects
autosave to readable local JSON files.

This short film shows the working Cedar House demo and the authoring Studio.
I also built a reusable capture and rendering workflow for the launch visuals.
